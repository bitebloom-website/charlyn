Who is BiteBloom for?

Students, office workers, families, and people who want to browse meals and practice a convenient online ordering journey.

The problem we address

Browsing scattered menus and calculating an order can feel confusing. BiteBloom organizes meal choices, prices, quantities, and order totals in one place.

Suggested user journey

Design challenge and solution

Challenge: Too many choices, unclear prices, and complicated checkout steps can confuse users.

Solution: BiteBloom uses categories, search, visible prices, quantity controls, a live cart total, readable labels, and a simple checkout form.
